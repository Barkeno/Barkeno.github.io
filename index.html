About

I am a researcher at SIASUN Robot & Automation Co., Ltd., one of China's leading robotics companies. My research focuses on LiDAR-inertial odometry, SLAM, and multi-sensor fusion for autonomous mobile robots operating in challenging real-world environments.

My work aims to build robust, real-time localization and mapping systems that can handle aggressive motion, sensor degradation, and long-term scene changes — pushing mobile robots toward reliable industrial deployment.

I am also interested in Visual-Language Navigation (VLN), exploring how robots can follow natural language instructions in complex environments by grounding language in spatial perception.

Projects

FR-LIO / Fast LiDAR-Inertial Odometry

A tightly-coupled LiDAR-inertial odometry system using iterated Kalman smoother and robocentric voxel map, achieving robust real-time localization under aggressive motion and sensor noise.

SLAM  |  C++  |  ROS

Long-Term SLAM with Change Detection

A graph-structured SLAM system with online map maintenance for long-term deployment. Uses a dual-layer octree representation to efficiently detect and adapt to environmental changes.

SLAM  |  C++  |  IEEE Sensors Journal

MULO: Multi-Landmark LiDAR Odometry

A LiDAR odometry approach that jointly optimizes over multiple geometric landmark types for improved accuracy and robustness in structured industrial environments.
