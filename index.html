About

I am a researcher at SIASUN Robot & Automation Co., Ltd., one of China's leading robotics companies. My research focuses on LiDAR-inertial odometry, SLAM, and multi-sensor fusion for autonomous mobile robots operating in challenging real-world environments.

I received my Ph.D. in Control Science and Engineering from Northeastern University (东北大学), China. My doctoral research centered on robust state estimation and localization for mobile robots, with a focus on tightly-coupled LiDAR-inertial systems.

My work aims to build robust, real-time localization and mapping systems that can handle aggressive motion, sensor degradation, and long-term scene changes — pushing mobile robots toward reliable industrial deployment. I am also interested in Visual-Language Navigation (VLN), exploring how robots can follow natural language instructions in complex environments.

Projects

FR-LIO / Fast LiDAR-Inertial Odometry

A tightly-coupled LiDAR-inertial odometry system using iterated Kalman smoother and robocentric voxel map, achieving robust real-time localization under aggressive motion and sensor noise.

SLAM  ·  C++  ·  ROS

Long-Term SLAM with Change Detection

A graph-structured SLAM system with online map maintenance for long-term deployment. Uses a dual-layer octree representation to detect and adapt to environmental changes.
